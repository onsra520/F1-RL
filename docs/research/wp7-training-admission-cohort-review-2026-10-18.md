## Registered experiment and current implementation

The rotating-owner registration fixes three seed bundles, four single-track cohorts and one multitrack cohort. `build_protocol()` creates one separately trained policy instance for each cohort/seed combination: **15 policy instances**, each evaluated on **four TRAIN tracks × two strata**. Thus the roster is **120 distinct instance/track/stratum cells**, with **60 valid episodes per cell, at least 7,200 valid episodes overall**. Single-track instances are evaluated on all four TRAIN tracks, including tracks outside that instance's training subset; this is not HELD-OUT access. See [registration](../../configs/research/wp7-rotating-owner-protocol-v3.json), [protocol construction](../../f1rl/research/wp7_protocol.py).

The evaluation registration retains stochastic actions, final-model primary analysis, diagnostic checkpoints without best-checkpoint selection, and abort-on-invalid with preserved evidence and no redraw/success credit. `evaluation_seed()` includes the **policy instance identity**, track, stratum and episode; policy-action RNG has a separate hash domain. Consequently matching training seed indices are paired training replicates, but different policy instances do **not** receive common evaluation episode seeds by construction. Matching episode numbers alone do not establish paired environmental draws. [Protocol](../../f1rl/research/wp7_protocol.py), [cell execution](../../f1rl/research/wp7_cell_evaluation.py).

The generic legacy `RobustnessCell` API has different semantics: its single `(policy_id, track_id, stratum_id)` row requires at least three `paired_seed_ids`, equal seed sets across that policy's strata, and one binomial success/episode count. It is not wired to the registered cell executor. A registered cell identifies **one trained seed-specific policy**, not three training seeds. Merely placing `(0,1,2)` in each row would falsely attest provenance; pooling three distinct trained policies into one ordinary binomial row additionally needs a justified common-probability/exchangeability estimand. Preserve the legacy API and evidence meaning; do not use its minimum-seed guard to redefine or silently triple the registered 120-cell roster. [Generic numerical gate](../../f1rl/research/wp7_robustness_gate.py), [legacy tests](../../tests/research/test_wp7_robustness_gate.py).

## Numerical meaning and multiplicity

For a fixed cell with binomial observations, the implementation solves `P(Binomial(n,L) >= x) = .05`, uses `L=0` for zero successes and `.05**(1/n)` for all successes. This agrees with the one-sided lower limit in R's primary implementation (`qbeta(alpha,x,n-x+1)`). Exact confidence is at least the nominal confidence under the binomial model; it is not a posterior probability of success. [R documentation](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-patched/library/stats/html/binom.test.html), [R Core implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wch/r-source/trunk/src/library/stats/R/binom.test.R).

Three distinct claims must not be conflated:

- **Per-cell coverage:** each prespecified lower limit has at least 95% marginal coverage under its binomial assumptions. This does not establish 95% simultaneous coverage for the vector of all 120 limits.
- **Single all-cells decision:** a prespecified conjunction requiring every cell to reject `p <= .90` can be an intersection-union test at level .05 without dividing alpha by 120. Direct argument: if any component null is true, rejecting all implies rejecting that true component, whose rejection probability is at most .05. This does not require independent cells and does not turn the 120 intervals into simultaneous confidence intervals. The clinical co-primary-endpoint analogue is explicitly distinguished from any-endpoint success in FDA's primary guidance; its statistical logic is relevant here, not its clinical regulatory authority. [FDA, sections III.C.1–2](https://www.fda.gov/media/162416/download).
- **Selective or comparative claims:** choosing a favorable policy/checkpoint, accepting any subset, asserting simultaneous interval coverage, or making cohort superiority claims changes the inferential target. Those claims need an explicitly reviewed analysis plan; the conjunction argument does not automatically cover them.

The current label `per_cell_no_familywise_claim_owner_review_required` therefore need not be a numerical defect. It leaves the intended scientific claim unresolved. Neither automatic Bonferroni correction nor automatic endorsement is justified. Keep **alpha .05, bound .90, 120 cells and >=7,200 episodes** unchanged pending the owner decision. Conditional episode-level inference for fixed checkpoints also does not establish performance over the population of future training seeds.
